Our Story

PT Recycling was founded with a simple mission: to provide fair, transparent pricing for recycled metals. What began as a small family operation has grown into the area's local metal recycling facilities.

With over a decade of experience in the industry, our team has developed expertise in processing all types of metals with a special focus on catalytic converters and their valuable precious metals.

Our Mission

We are committed to:

  • Providing the highest possible value for your recyclable metals
  • Operating with complete transparency in our pricing and processes
  • Reducing environmental impact through responsible recycling practices
