About PT Recycling

Your trusted partner in metal and catalytic converter recycling since 2005.

Our Story

PT Recycling was founded with a simple mission: to provide fair, transparent pricing for recycled metals while promoting environmental sustainability. What began as a small family operation has grown into one of the region's most trusted metal recycling facilities.

With decades of combined experience in the recycling industry, our team has developed expertise in processing all types of metals with a special focus on catalytic converters and their valuable precious metals.

Our Mission

We are committed to:

  • Providing the highest possible value for your recyclable metals
  • Operating with complete transparency in our pricing and processes
  • Reducing environmental impact through responsible recycling practices
  • Supporting our local community through job creation and sustainable business practices

Our Team

Our Facility

Our state-of-the-art recycling facility features the latest technology for processing and evaluating metals of all types. We maintain strict environmental and safety standards that exceed industry requirements.

Certifications & Compliance

PT Recycling maintains all required state and federal licenses for metal recycling operations. We are proud members of the Institute of Scrap Recycling Industries (ISRI) and adhere to their code of ethics and environmental standards.
